## Overview: Vedika vs AstrologyAPI.com in 2026

AstrologyAPI.com is an established Vedic astrology computation API with approximately 50+ endpoints covering birth charts, dashas, doshas, panchang, and kundali matching. It focuses purely on structured data output — planetary positions, house cusps, dasha periods — and leaves interpretation to the developer.

Vedika, built (NVIDIA Inception Partner), provides 700+ operations across three astrology systems (Vedic, Western, KP) plus an AI chatbot that generates natural language astrological readings with precision verification. Where AstrologyAPI.com returns raw JSON data, Vedika can return both structured data and conversational interpretations.

## Where AstrologyAPI.com Wins

- **Established market presence** — AstrologyAPI.com has been serving developers longer and has a larger existing user base.
- **Simpler integration** — If you only need basic Vedic computation data, AstrologyAPI.com's focused API may be simpler to integrate.

## Where Vedika Wins

- **AI chatbot with precision-verified AI** — Vedika can generate natural language astrological readings from birth data. Every claim is validated against XALEN Ephemeris computed data. AstrologyAPI.com has no AI layer.
- **3 astrology systems** — Vedika covers Vedic + Western + KP in one API. AstrologyAPI.com is Vedic-only. If your app needs synastry, progressions, or KP analysis, Vedika is the only option.
- **37% cheaper** — Vedika starts at $12/month vs AstrologyAPI.com's $19/month.
- **30 languages** — Critical for apps targeting Indian regional markets (Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Marathi, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam) plus international markets.
- **Deeper Vedic coverage** — 60 divisional charts, 6 dasha systems, 300+ yoga detection vs AstrologyAPI.com's more limited set.
- **Published accuracy and uptime** — sub-arcsecond birth-chart precision and uptime SLA, verified over 6 months of production data.

## When to Choose AstrologyAPI.com

AstrologyAPI.com is suitable if you need only basic Vedic computation data (birth charts, dashas, doshas), prefer a longer-established provider, and do not need AI interpretation, Western astrology, KP analysis, or multi-language support.

## When to Choose Vedika

Vedika is the better choice if you need conversational AI (chatbots, personalized readings), Western or KP astrology, multi-language support for Indian and international markets, or deep Vedic coverage (60 divisional charts, 6 dasha systems, 300+ yogas). At $12/month vs $19/month, Vedika also offers better value for equivalent computation features.
