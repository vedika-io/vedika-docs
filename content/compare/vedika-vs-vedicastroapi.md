## Where Vedika Wins

- **4.7x more endpoints** — 600+ vs ~30+. Vedika covers three astrology systems in one API.
- **AI chatbot with precision-verified AI** — Natural language astrological readings validated against computed data. VedicAstroAPI has no AI.
- **40% cheaper** — $12/month vs $20/month for the starter tier.
- **30 languages** — 11 Indian languages + 19 international. VedicAstroAPI is English-only.
- **XALEN Ephemeris precision** — Sub-arcsecond accuracy with 8 ayanamsa systems. Published sub-arcsecond JPL DE440 agreement.
- **Western + KP astrology** — Synastry, composite charts, progressions, solar return, KP sub-lord analysis.

## When to Choose VedicAstroAPI

VedicAstroAPI may be suitable if you need only basic Vedic computation data and are already integrated with their system. However, with fewer endpoints, no AI, no Western/KP support, no multi-language, and a higher price point, VedicAstroAPI offers less value per dollar than Vedika.

## When to Choose Vedika

Vedika is the better choice for any new astrology project. It offers more endpoints, more systems, AI capabilities, multi-language support, published accuracy metrics, and a lower price. The free sandbox makes it easy to evaluate before committing.
